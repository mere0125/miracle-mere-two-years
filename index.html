HOW TO PLAY

Not quite ordinary Flying Chess.

  1. Roll the dice and follow the stitched path.
  2. Cross a gift space to collect its clue, even if you do not land on it.
  3. Magic spaces can boost you. Clouds may send you back.
  4. Every turn adds a memory or activity card. You will finish every card before the final surprise.
  5. Choices earn Heart Sparks. Spend 3 to cancel a detour.
  6. Your pet guide gives you one special power.
  7. You need an exact roll for the last space. Extra steps take you back along the path.
